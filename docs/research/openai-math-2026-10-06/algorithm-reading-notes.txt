## Main finding

No manuscript in this range establishes a drop-in improvement to PETLS's persistent Schur-complement eigensolver or preservation of harmonic localization under sparsification. The best scoped expansion is a degree-preserving graph-null-model analysis inspired by 131. The next most relevant are mathematical benchmark families (177,178), with substantial construction/field caveats. 174 is especially easy to overclaim: cut-thin trees are NOT spectral sparsifiers or homology-preserving replacements.

## 131 — degree-preserving topology/spectrum null ensembles (strongest feature candidate)

Paper: [Polynomial mixing of the switch chain for every graphical degree sequence](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Polynomial-Mixing-of-the-Switch-Chain-for-Every-Graphical-Degree-Sequence-September-25-2026/main.pdf).

Theorem 1.1, p1: for each labelled graphical degree vector on n>=4 vertices, its precise lazy undirected simple-graph switch chain has TV mixing time <=2n^8 at tolerance 1/4, and spectral gap >=1/[24n² binom(n,4)] when the realization space is not singleton. Transition: hold with probability 1/2; otherwise choose a four-vertex set and one of six ordered pairs of distinct perfect matchings; execute only if old edges exist and new edges do not. Sections 2–7 compare this to symmetry-preserving two-vertex heat baths using projection inequalities. Corollary 1.2/Section 8 assert an exactly uniform sampler with expected polynomial bit time.

Application (our inference): a `degree_preserving_null` example/analysis module would compare observed graph Betti curves, smallest positive spectra and localized harmonic edge energy with graphs preserving every labelled degree. This answers whether higher topology and spectra reveal organization beyond the degree sequence. Feed sampled incidence/clique complexes into existing `Complex`/simplex-tree paths; the spectral math need not change. A useful output is empirical quantiles or tail fractions for a predeclared statistic, with seed, chain definition, number of proposals, acceptance rate and sampling assurance recorded.

Scope limits: unweighted undirected simple graphs on complete host, no connectivity condition. Directed dFlag, edge weights, geometry-restricted edges, or connected-only rejection chains are not covered. For weighted/scale-dependent graphs, independent rewiring at each scale need not form a nested filtration; define a null model of the underlying data or use clearly separate per-scale comparisons. It is not a molecular trajectory ensemble or chemically valid molecular generator. n^8 is a theoretical bound, unusable as a default numerical burn-in at ordinary sizes. Ordinary edge swaps already existed; novelty is the uniform all-degree-sequence guarantee. A short run must not be advertised as certified uniform or automatically produce valid independent-sample p-values.

Formalization: [lean/docs/131.md](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/lean/docs/131.md) covers mixing, connectivity, exponential TV estimates and gap. **Explicitly excludes the exact-uniform sampler**. I did not run the Lean toolchain.

## 177 — bounded-degree high-dimensional topology benchmarks (research/fixture candidate)

Paper: [Bounded-degree coboundary expanders in every dimension](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Bounded-degree-coboundary-expanders-in-every-dimension-September-24-2026/paper.pdf).

Theorem 1.1 pp1–2: for each d>=3, connected pure d-complexes X_m with vertex counts tending to infinity, bounded top-face incidence per vertex, and `||δ_i f|| >= ε dist(f,B^i)` for 0<=i<d, over F2 with normalized top-face-induced Hamming weights. This forces reduced F2 cohomology below d to vanish. Section 2 builds X_m as the d-skeleton of a coset complex of congruence quotients of SL_(2d+1)(F_q[t]), modulo t^m, q a sufficiently large fixed odd prime; Section 5 transfers cosystolic inequalities and proves the required vanishing. Constants may depend on d.

Application: sparse, increasing-size high-dimensional benchmark complexes with a theorem-backed expected F2 kernel. Validate boundary composition, distinguish genuine from numerical nullity, measure positive-mode performance in large complexes. Since finite-complex rational Betti ranks are bounded above by F2 Betti dimensions, the vanishing also implies reduced real homology vanishes below d. But there is **no asserted numerical real Laplacian spectral gap** from this weighted F2 inequality alone, nor a result about a filtration's intermediate/persistent groups.

Critical existing-code mismatch: Gudhi is hardcoded to F11. F2 acyclicity does NOT rule out odd torsion detectable over F11. Use an independent F2 check or explicitly expose coefficient-field choice before claiming a zero authoritative Betti fixture. Real harmonic validation remains separate. The coset construction may be enormous at its first permitted parameters; paper provides no PETLS-sized implementation or practical size/cost bound. Start from explicit small checkable instances only if construction is feasible; do not make full implementation a default roadmap priority.

Formalization: [lean/docs/177.md](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/lean/docs/177.md) covers existence in d>=3 with field/weights as above. Graph and d=2 inputs are separate. This does not certify PyTorch real numerics.

## 178 — ordinary graph Laplacian benchmark fixtures (moderate utility, low novelty for library)

Paper: [Deterministic nonbipartite Ramanujan graphs in every fixed degree](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Deterministic-nonbipartite-Ramanujan-graphs-in-every-fixed-degree-September-23-2026/paper.pdf).

Theorem 1.1 p3 constructs a simple d-regular graph for each fixed d>=3 and every sufficiently large even n with all nonconstant adjacency eigenvalues strictly in (-2sqrt(d-1),2sqrt(d-1)); adjacency list output costs O_d(n^k_d) bit operations. Threshold and exponent depend on d. The long construction uses deterministic configuration exposure and exact local spectral repair (Section 8); no practical crossover or local-neighbor oracle is asserted.

By L0=dI-A (our elementary deduction), its positive eigenvalues lie strictly in `(d-2sqrt(d-1), d+2sqrt(d-1))`, with exactly one zero. A pure graph incidence complex also has beta1=m-n+1 and the same nonzero spectrum for B1^T B1 and B1 B1^T; this is a clean large-kernel/range-mode solver test. Use the 1-dimensional graph complex: clique/Rips completion may add triangles and change H1. Preexisting explicit expanders already offer much of this benefit; novelty is coverage of every fixed degree and all sufficiently large even orders. No persistent-spectrum/localization bound follows.

No Lean link in supplied catalogue; fetching lean/docs/178.md returned 404. Treat manuscript claims as unformalized at pinned snapshot, unless parent finds other artifacts.

## 089/094 — metric preprocessing research, not ready performance patches

089: [planar](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Planar-Graph-Metrics-Embed-into-L1-with-Constant-Distortion-September-23-2026/paper.pdf), Thm1.1 p2, and [bounded treewidth](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/L1-Embeddings-of-Graphs-of-Bounded-Treewidth-September-23-2026/paper.pdf), Thm1.1 p2: finite connected positive-length undirected graph shortest-path metrics embed in real L1 with distortion C universal for planar or C(k) for bag-size k. Proofs construct multiscale random cut/coordinate distributions; not a small fast GPU coordinate map. Could motivate a shortest-path-metric Rips interface, but existing `Rips(distances=...)` already computes exact graph-metric persistence, so embedding is unnecessary to unlock that capability.

094: [Subpolynomial dimension reduction in Lp](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Subpolynomial-dimension-reduction-in-Lp-September-23-2026/paper.pdf), Thm1.1 pp1–2: fixed 1<p<infinity,p!=2, D>1, embedding dimension <=exp(C_p,D(log n)^gamma), gamma=2-p below2 and 1-2/p above2. Exact embeddings have worst-case quadratic dimension. **Explicitly existential; no efficient embedding algorithm asserted.** p=2 already has stronger classical JL log n dimension. p=1 excluded, so cannot chain 089 into 094.

For an actual map with `d <= d' <= Dd`, elementary inclusions are `Rips_d(r/D) subset Rips_d'(r) subset Rips_d(r)` on identical labelled vertices, giving multiplicative scale control. This is an inference, not a persistent-Laplacian theorem in these papers. It does not bound spectral or individual harmonic-representative errors; those require spectral gaps and operator comparisons. L1/Lp images fed to `points=` would silently use the wrong Euclidean metric. No new Alpha geometry guarantee follows.

Lean scope docs 089 and 094 match existence/distortion claims, not practical algorithms.

## 174 — watchlist proof methods; reject claimed direct eigensolver acceleration

Papers: [The strong thin tree conjecture](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/The-strong-thin-tree-conjecture-September-23-2026/paper.pdf), [A polynomial-time construction of strong thin trees](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-polynomial-time-construction-of-strong-thin-trees-September-23-2026/paper.pdf).

Existence Thm1.1 p1 / algorithm Thm1.1 p2: loopless finite k-edge-connected multigraph has spanning tree with each cut at most C/k times original cut, deterministic polynomial binary-input algorithm even for binary multiplicities. Existence paper p4 explicitly: a cut inequality need not be semidefinite. Proof uses shortcut positive matrices, low effective resistance, matroid/tree packing and matrix-signing sparsification; algorithm uses ellipsoid optimization and finite-bit normalization. These are possible proof inspirations, not established higher-order solver tools.

There is no `(1-epsilon)L_G <= L_H <= (1+epsilon)L_G` guarantee and no low-stretch/condition-number estimate for the final tree. H0 connectivity survives but graph H1 is deleted. For example a cycle has beta1=1; any spanning tree has beta1=0. Replacing a complex skeleton by the tree is incompatible with persistent topology. Classical spectral sparsification/preconditioning requires different theorems and, for q>0/persistent Schur complements/sheaves, extra structure. Lean docs cover both thinness and algorithmic result; no numerical C claimed in existence statement.

## 113 follow-up — allowed-edge degree-preserving null ensembles (stronger research feature scope)

At parent request followed the cross-reference from 131 to the actual [FPRAS perfect-matchings paper](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-Fully-Polynomial-Randomized-Approximation-Scheme-for-Perfect-Matchings-in-General-Graphs-September-23-2026/main.pdf), **Corollary10.1 and complete Sections10.1–10.2 pp41–44**. Downloaded/extracted 45 pages; this raises total downloaded to 15 manuscripts/550 pages. Companion entropy paper not inspected beyond catalogue description.

Cor10.1: for explicit simple undirected unweighted host G=(V,E), binary degree requirements f, family A={F subset E: deg_F(v)=f_v}, it (i) approximately counts A with arbitrary relative error/confidence and exact empty detection, and (ii) reports infeasibility exactly or returns feasible F on every execution with output law TV<=tau from uniform. Time is polynomial in full input length and tau^-1 on every run. No exact or pointwise almost-uniform sampling, edge weights or compressed multiplicities is claimed. Analogous result holds for fixed-cardinality matching.

Reduction details matter: replace each edge incidence by a tagged port, link two ports for each original edge, and add d_v-f_v labelled dummy vertices adjacent to all v-ports. Gadget has <=4m vertices and <=m+2m(n-1) edges. Every f-factor has the same fiber cardinality product_v(d_v-f_v)!, so uniform perfect matchings project uniformly. Sampling uses count-guided edge deletion with exact perfect-matching feasibility at every branch (Section10.2), controlling total-variation accumulation; deterministic projection cannot increase TV. This is substantially more concrete than a vague relation to matching theory.

Our application inference: a user-specified allowed-edge mask (for example permissible spatial contacts) could preserve degree counts and forbidden contacts while randomizing higher organization, followed by existing topology/spectrum computations. This is a better mathematically supported research direction than assuming 131's complete-host switch chain mixes on a restricted host. It still does not enforce connectivity, weights, directed edges, temporal dynamics, nested multiscale filtrations or chemical plausibility, and its polynomial bound supplies no practical implementation/runtime promise. Treat it as a research prototype after validating the count/sampling machinery, not a near-term ready backend.

Formalization scope [113](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/lean/docs/113.md) explicitly lists the perfect-matching FPRAS count on all random tapes, and separate entropy results. It does not specifically list the host-constrained degree sampler or Cor10.1; that corollary was checked in the mathematical paper. Do not label the whole sampler Lean-certified.
